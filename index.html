YOU

Your only enemy: you, a moment ago.

What is it?

Déjà You is a one-thumb arcade game for iPhone and iPad. You guide Vu to collect lights. Every light you grab turns the path you just took into a copy of you that runs it back and forth. Touch a copy and the run ends.

Help

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the screen to move Vu. Grab the yellow light before its ring runs out. Avoid the pink copies. The white diamond wipes nearby copies. Every 10 lights starts a new wave and gives you a short moment of safety.

I changed phones. Where is my progress?

Progress (level, sparks, skins, best scores) is backed up with your own iCloud account. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new device, make sure iCloud is turned on, and open the game. The Profile screen shows whether backup is active.

I bought “Plus” or “No Ads” but it is not active.

Open the shop from the spark counter on the home screen and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a single device. Spark packs are consumable and are restored only through the iCloud backup above.

Can I play without ads?

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to continue a run, double a reward or get an extra daily try. Ads between runs can be removed with “No Ads”. “Plus” removes all ads and gives those rewards without watching.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off the switch for Déjà You. The game works exactly the same. See the privacy policy for details.

The leaderboards do not open.

Leaderboards use Apple’s Game Center. Sign in at Settings → Game Center, then open Profile → Leaderboards in the game.
