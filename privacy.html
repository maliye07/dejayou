Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Déjà You (“the game”) uses and who processes it. The game is published by Akif Fakıoğlu (“we”).

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • We do not run our own servers for the game and do not receive your gameplay data.
  • Ads and purchases are provided by third parties (Google and Apple, with RevenueCat for purchase validation). They process some device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: level, experience, sparks, unlocked skins, best scores, daily mission progress, settings and the nickname you may type on the Profile screen. This data stays on your device unless you use the backup described next.

iCloud backup

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, the same progress data is saved to your own iCloud key-value storage so that it can be restored on a new device. This storage belongs to your Apple Account. We cannot read it. You can turn it off or delete it in the iCloud settings of your device.

Game Center

Leaderboards are provided by Apple’s Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple and shown under your Game Center name according to your Game Center settings. If your nickname in the game is empty, the game may copy your Game Center name into it on your device. Game Center is optional.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data.

On iOS the game asks for your permission before using the advertising identifier for tracking (“Allow Tracking”). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you are asked for consent before personalized ads are used.

Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them, the game uses RevenueCat, which processes purchase receipts, product identifiers, an anonymous app user identifier, and device and app information. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics

The game does not include an analytics service of our own. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as downloads and crashes) if you have agreed to share them with developers in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Deleting the game removes the data stored on your device.
  • The iCloud backup can be removed in your device’s iCloud settings.
  • For data held by Google, Apple or RevenueCat, you can use their own tools, or contact us and we will pass your request on where we are able to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, the quickest route is usually the settings described above; you can also contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
